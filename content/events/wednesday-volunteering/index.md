Every Wednesday morning, from 09:00 to 12:00, we carry out planned volunteer tasks around Telford Town Park. Recent tasks include restoring the Chelsea and Maxel gardens, painting benches and clearing overhanging trees from footpaths — see [Our Projects](/our-projects/) for more.

There's no subscription to pay, and you're welcome to come along and help as and when you're available.

Want to join in, or not sure where to meet? [Get in touch](/contact-us/) and we'll point you in the right direction. If you'd like to join us properly, you can also [become a member](/become-a-member/).
